Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026

Brief does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no network connection of any kind.

What this version does

This release of Brief displays a sample brief that ships inside the app. All of its content is fabricated and is bundled with the app itself.

The app:

  • makes no network requests
  • contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • creates no account and asks for no sign-in
  • requests no system permissions
  • stores nothing about you, on the device or anywhere else

Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing to share, sell, disclose, or delete, and no third party receives any information about you.

Future versions

If a future version of Brief connects to a device you own in order to display your own brief, that processing is designed to happen on your own hardware. This policy will be updated before any such version is released, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.

Children

Brief is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Support requests can be sent to the contact address listed on the app's App Store page.
