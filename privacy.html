Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Brief does not collect data. It has no account, no analytics, and no ads.

Where briefs come from

Briefs come from the Brief shortcut, which runs in the Shortcuts app. It finds today's messages and summarizes them with the model in its Use Model step. Brief receives only that summary.

Data on your iPhone

Brief keeps the last 14 days of briefs, your triage (done, snoozed, pinned), your settings, and a log of when the shortcut ran with counts only. All of it stays on your iPhone. Brief has no server and shares nothing with anyone. Erase briefs in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything.

Permissions

Notifications: to tell you when a brief is ready, and for the optional reminder.
Contacts: read on your iPhone only, to address a reply.

You can turn either off in iOS Settings.
