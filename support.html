What Brief does

Brief turns a day of messages into a short, ranked worklist: what needs you in the next ten minutes, what can wait until today, and what is already handled.

Conversations are ordered by when they actually need you. Items further down the list are dimmed rather than hidden, so the top of the list is where your eye lands first.

This version

This release ships with a sample brief so you can see how it reads and how the ranking behaves. It makes no network connections and collects no data.

Frequently asked

Does Brief read my messages?

No. This version displays only the sample brief bundled inside the app. It has no access to Messages and makes no network requests.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in and no account.

Why is there no badge or unread count?

Because the ranking is the product. Priority is shown by position and emphasis instead of alerts, so the list stays readable.

Why is part of the list faded?

Fading indicates lower urgency. Everything is still there and still tappable — it is simply ordered by when it needs you.
