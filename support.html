Brief for iPhone

What Brief does

Brief turns a day of messages into a short, ranked worklist: who is waiting on you and what they asked for. Mark conversations done, snooze or pin them, look back over two weeks, and reply from a drafted message.

Requirements

iOS 27 or later.

Setup

  1. Open Brief and allow notifications.
  2. In Settings, tap Add the Brief shortcut.
  3. Tap Make a brief now and allow Shortcuts access when asked.
  4. In Shortcuts > Automation, add a Time of Day automation that runs the Brief shortcut.
