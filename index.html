A ranked daily worklist

Brief for iPhone

Brief turns a day of messages into a short, ranked worklist: what needs you in the next ten minutes, what can wait until today, and what is already handled.

Conversations are ordered by when they actually need you. Items further down the list are dimmed rather than hidden, so the top of the list is where your eye lands first. There are no unread badges and no counts to clear.

Brief works with a shortcut you add from the app.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 27 or later.
